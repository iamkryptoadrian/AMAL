01 — The Problem

Faith and livelihood were never meant to compete.

Young Muslims are quietly asked to choose between time for the dīn and time for the dunyā. We are rich in intention, yet short on continuity, and three compounding gaps keep it that way. No single tool addresses them together.

Design principle

Three gaps, one missing bridge.

١The personal gap

Spiritual

Consistency is difficult.

A daily practice is easy to value and hard to sustain amid work, study and family.

  • Motivation that peaks in Ramadan often fades after Eid.
  • Habit apps that gamify publicly risk riyā’ (showing off) and quiet shame.
To test with user interviews

Illustrative shape, not measured data.

٢The livelihood gap

Economic

Earning rarely honours values.

Young Muslims looking for flexible income find few options designed around their principles.

  • Most micro-task and gig platforms ignore riba, gambling and content concerns.
  • Earnings are tied to harvesting attention, not to meaningful work.
To test with target users

Illustrative comparison, not measured data.

٣The funding gap

Structural

Good programs need support.

Masjids, waqf trusts and community sponsors want to fund learning, but lack a transparent, measurable channel.

  • An artificial split between spiritual growth and financial sustainability.
  • Institutions have budgets and intent, but no privacy-safe way to see outcomes.
To test sponsor willingness to pay
02 — The Product

Built like a masjid: trust at the foundation.

A daily ritual, and a fairer reward. AMAL is private by design and sponsor-funded by principle, so rewards stay honest, dignified and free of public comparison. The trust gap is closed by architecture, not by promise.

Funded Rewards

Small, real rewards (around $0.20 per verified event in our illustrative model) from programs that are fully funded before anything is promised. Paid through licensed rails, never in points that expire.

04 — Revenue Model

Four ethical streams. Zero user fees.

Institutions pay for outcomes they already value: consistent learning, trusted reach and privacy-safe insight. Users only ever receive. Rewards are a cost we fund, never a margin we take.

Users Never Pay. Monetization is 100% Institutional & Ethical.

Let purpose pay for purpose.

The principle is non-negotiable: partners fund programs before user rewards are promised.
